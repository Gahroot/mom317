## Brand facts (from 317 brand vision.pdf)
- Hero product: middle-of-the-night sleep support for pre/peri/post-menopausal women who wake after 3–4 h and can't fall back asleep. Name 3:17 = the time you wake.
- Founder: 15 years of this problem herself; built what she couldn't find.
- Values: ingredient transparency; make someone's life better; truthful (no hype, no fear); faith-guided ("Approved by God").
- Roadmap: Cool (hot flashes), Calm (evening), Travel (jet lag), Restore (morning). -> waitlist can preview the "system".
- Community: women who feel dismissed; want to be seen. 5-yr goal 1M women; giving/advocacy.
- Packaging: amber bottle, sand label, gold "3:17" wordmark, black product copy.
