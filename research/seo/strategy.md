# Back2SleepNow.com: content strategy (working doc)

Goal: qualified waitlist signups (women in peri/post-menopause who wake in the night and can't get back to sleep), plus visibility in Google and AI answers (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews). Byline: Marian Grout, founder, "a woman who spent 15 years awake at 3 a.m." No other biography.

Research date: 25 Oct 2026. SERP notes come from live web searches that day. Keyword volumes are **not measured** (we have no Ahrefs/Semrush access), so demand is rated relative (H/M/L) from SERP crowding and phrasing. Check them in a keyword tool before scaling.

## 3. Gaps we can win

1. **Evidence-first answers to the 3 a.m. question.** The top results argue cortisol vs. blood sugar with little citation. We cite the actual sleep-lab work: hot flushes coincide with ~69% of awakenings (de Zambotti 2014), plus the SWAN and Baker reviews, and we say plainly what's unknown.
2. **The "right now, at 3:17" moment.** Few pages are written for the woman reading on her phone in the dark. Short, calm, do-this-now formats (dark-mode friendly, one-screen answer box) fit both the brand and the intent.
3. **Founder voice.** Clinics and publishers can't say "I've been there". Marian can, within the single fact we have.
4. **Honest supplement content.** Brands (Bonafide, Kindra) sell. The Menopause Society 2023 statement doesn't recommend supplements for hot flushes. A brand that says so earns trust and AI citations, but it needs careful legal review.
5. **CBT-I made concrete for menopause.** Strong evidence (McCurry 2016), yet rarely offered to women, and almost no pages translate it into night-time steps.

## 6. On-page and AEO checklist

40-60 word answer box under the H1; question H2s that match the real query wording; 4-6 FAQs (FAQ schema); a visible "Sources" list with DOI/PubMed links; byline plus "medically reviewed by" once a reviewer exists (recommended: hire a credentialed reviewer, which is a big E-E-A-T lift); the updated date shown; one soft in-text waitlist mention at most.
